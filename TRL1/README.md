# TRL1 — Scientific principles and problem definition

**Evidence status:** retrospectively supported scientific foundation; formal historical acceptance date not verified.

**Objective:** define the scientific need, measurement principle, validity domain, and research questions for 3D geometry-based assessment of welded joints.

## Scientific basis
- A reference workpiece/CAD geometry can be compared with a scanned welded surface after registration.
- A spatial deviation field may indicate some surface geometry anomalies.
- Surface data alone do **not** prove the absence of internal defects, fine cracks, or compliance with weld acceptance criteria.

## Research questions
1. Which surface deviations are meaningfully observable by the proposed optical measurement chain?
2. How do registration uncertainty and point-cloud quality affect a deviation map?
3. Which defect classes cannot be inferred reliably from external geometry alone?
4. What reference, calibration, and traceability metadata are required?
