# Evidence provenance and reporting rules

This ZIP was assembled on 2026-10-15 from a previously prepared TRL1–TRL7 technical draft and referenced publication records. **These Markdown files are modern reconstructions.** No original 2021–2024 signed TRL acceptance logs were supplied with this ZIP.

1. Distinguish publication date from acceptance date.
2. Distinguish one verified subsystem from a complete integrated demonstrator.
3. Distinguish raw measured data, reprocessed data and synthetic examples.
4. Link primary publications by DOI; publishing manuscript PDFs or proprietary raw data requires permission.
5. Reconstructed/modern code must retain its actual version history and may not be backdated.
6. Requirements and diagrams are engineering documentation, not test-pass certificates.
7. For formal reports, cross-check exact publication titles, author lists and institutional identifiers with originals.
8. TRL4 validation remains separate and partly outstanding; TRL1–3 documentation does not imply TRL4 closure.
