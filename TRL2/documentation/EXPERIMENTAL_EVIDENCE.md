TRL2 – Experimental Research Evidence

Evidence status

Retrospectively documented research evidence supporting the technology concept. The experimental results are historical research outputs and are not presented as newly executed tests.

Reference geometry and investigated samples

The study compares an ideal reference weld geometry (idealis a3) against multiple geometric variants:

amn_a3B, amn_a3C, amn_a3D

amk_a3C, amk_a3D

aszim_a3B, aszim_a3C, aszim_a3D

Documented processing methodology

Point cloud generation from reference and investigated geometries.

Point cloud registration using FGR and ICP.

Geometric feature analysis using FPFH.

Distance distribution and histogram analysis.

Identification and visualization of geometric deviations.

Evaluation of error regions and their relationship to weld quality requirements.

Available research evidence

TRL2_HKJ_bővebb.docx – technology concept and methodological justification.

trl_2_eredmények_hkj_0427.docx – geometric comparisons, figures, distance distributions and numerical results.

trl_2_pontfelhők(1).zip – associated geometrical assets, internal content pending verification.

HJIC 2021 and Sensors 2023 – related published research.

Limitations

The documented experiments do not by themselves establish real-time Digital Twin operation or full production-level validation.

The historical findings must be distinguished from new reproducibility tests, and the original Python source files and geometrical assets must be checked before claiming complete computational reproduction.

Transition towards TRL3

The documented geometric methodology provides a foundation for proof-of-concept testing with real scanned workpieces, algorithmic evaluation and robot process data.
