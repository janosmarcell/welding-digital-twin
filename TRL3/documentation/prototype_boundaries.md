# PoC boundaries and temporal provenance

- 3D point-cloud registration/localization and welding-process recording are **separate** research demonstrations.
- No claim of an online production quality controller, real-time integrated twin, automatic robot reprogramming, or certified ISO weld classification is made.
- A surface scan is not sufficient evidence of absence of internal weld flaws.
- Software first reconstructed in 2026 should carry the new creation date and explicit source citations.
- The TRL4 `data/`, `src/` and `validation/` results should be linked rather than copied and re-labelled as earlier experiments.
