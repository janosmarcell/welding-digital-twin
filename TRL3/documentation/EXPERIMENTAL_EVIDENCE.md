TRL3 – Experimental Proof-of-Concept Evidence

1. Objective

Experimental verification of robotic welding data acquisition and geometric deviation analysis using real scanned welded workpieces.

2. Research evidence

The TRL3 research includes two complementary proof-of-concept activities:

A. Three-dimensional geometric evaluation

Structured-light scanning of real welded workpieces.

Comparison against an ideal reference geometry.

Evaluation of three defective workpiece models.

Point cloud registration and geometric deviation assessment.

Investigation of the limitations of defect detection.

B. Robotic welding process data analysis

Acquisition and processing of robot welding time-series data.

Identification of welding-active periods using TorchSignal.

Extraction of segment-based characteristics.

Preliminary anomaly analysis.

5. Validation status

Published research provides evidence of functional proof-of-concept components.

Independent reproduction of all original software results remains subject to source-code and dataset verification.

6. Transition to TRL4

TRL3 establishes the individual geometric analysis and process-data foundations.

TRL4 extends these foundations towards laboratory-level data integration, repeatability assessment and module validation.
